Protocol: NEXUS

Automated Spaced Repetition System (ASRS)

01. The Theory

The human brain is designed to forget. This is known as the "Forgetting Curve". Without intervention, you will lose approx 50% of new information within 24 hours.

NEXUS interrupts this decay. By reviewing information at precise, calculating intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days...), we trick the brain into moving data from Short-Term Memory to Long-Term Storage.

02. How to Use

STEP 1: INPUT Add a topic you learned today. The system sets the status to "Level 0".
STEP 2: WAIT Do not study the topic immediately. Wait for the NEXUS dashboard to flag it as DUE.
STEP 3: REVIEW When a card is DUE, click "Review". This opens your calendar to schedule a revision session.

If you pass: The interval increases (e.g., see you in 3 days).
If you fail: The level drops (see you tomorrow).
