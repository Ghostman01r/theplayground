01. The Decay Algorithm

In 1885, Hermann Ebbinghaus discovered the Forgetting Curve. His data proved that human memory is not a storage box, but a leaking bucket.

The Statistic: You will forget approximately 50-80% of all new information within 24 hours unless you actively interrupt the decay.

FIG 1.0: RETENTION OVER TIME

The Solution: Spaced Repetition. By reviewing information at calculatingly increasing intervals, we flatten the curve. NEXUS automates this math for you.
